# Vision

CivicLens will become a trusted public-intelligence layer that makes government finances understandable at every level.

## Near-term vision

Build a working Port St. Lucie pilot that can answer core questions about city revenue, property taxes, departmental spending, capital projects, debt, contracts, and reported outcomes using official records.

## Long-term vision

A resident in any participating jurisdiction should be able to:

- Follow public revenue from source to fund, program, vendor, project, and outcome when the records permit it.
- Compare budgeted amounts with actual spending.
- Understand which dollars are restricted, transferred, borrowed, reserved, or available for general use.
- See who authorized major decisions and when.
- Review government claims using consistent AEN evidence.
- Download the underlying records and reproduce the analysis.

## Platform vision

CivicLens should be reusable rather than hard-coded for one city. Each jurisdiction will have its own source adapters and accounting map, but the core platform will share:

- Source registry
- Document ingestion
- Financial normalization
- Entity resolution
- Evidence graph
- Question-answering system
- AEN Review engine
- Public dashboard
- Audit trail

## What success looks like

CivicLens succeeds when:

1. Residents can understand public finances without specialized training.
2. Every material answer can be traced back to evidence.
3. Public officials can identify missing, inconsistent, or outdated information faster.
4. Journalists and researchers spend less time finding records and more time evaluating them.
5. Better information leads to better decisions, reduced waste, and stronger public trust.

## What CivicLens is not

CivicLens is not a partisan scoring machine, an accusation generator, or a substitute for an official audit. It is an independent evidence and explanation system designed to make public records usable.
