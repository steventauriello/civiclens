# Core Principles

These principles govern product design, data handling, AI behavior, public explanations, and AEN Reviews.

## 1. Evidence before conclusions

Every material financial claim must cite an official source or be labeled as an inference. Unsupported claims are not published.

## 2. Show the evidence

Every answer should provide a path to the source document, relevant page or record, retrieval date, and transformation history.

## 3. Accounting context matters

CivicLens must not flatten unlike concepts. Budget, appropriation, encumbrance, expenditure, payment, transfer, reserve, debt proceeds, and fund balance must remain distinct.

## 4. Missing data is not misconduct

A missing document, unexplained variance, or unavailable transaction is a transparency gap. It is not proof of fraud, waste, or abuse.

## 5. Facts, analysis, and opinion are separate

The interface must visibly distinguish:

- Official fact
- Computed result
- AI explanation
- Inference
- User opinion

## 6. AEN is a review, not a predetermined verdict

AEN organizes evidence about Accountability, Effectiveness, and Need. It should identify supported findings, unanswered questions, and missing evidence rather than produce a partisan grade.

## 7. Corrections remain visible

Corrections must include what changed, why it changed, when it changed, and which answers were affected.

## 8. Privacy by design

CivicLens focuses on public institutional finance. Personal data should not be collected or exposed unless it is lawfully public, necessary to the public-interest purpose, and handled with appropriate safeguards.

## 9. Security is part of trust

Source integrity, document hashes, access controls, secret management, dependency security, and audit logs are required product features.

## 10. Plain English without false simplicity

Complex accounting should be explained clearly without stripping away qualifications that change the meaning.

## 11. Reproducibility

A researcher should be able to reproduce a published number from the cited source and documented transformation.

## 12. Political independence

CivicLens follows evidence regardless of which party, official, institution, program, or policy benefits from the result.

## 13. Official records remain authoritative

CivicLens is an independent interpretation layer. It does not replace official financial systems, audits, legal records, or agency determinations.

## 14. Residents come first

Features are judged by whether they help ordinary people answer meaningful questions about public money.
