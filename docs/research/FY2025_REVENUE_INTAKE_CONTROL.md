## First Tax-and-Service-Charge Subtotal

The first clearly identified tax-and-service-charge categories add to:

```text
Property taxes                 $110,632,000
Other taxes                     $82,754,000
Charges for services           $224,541,000
------------------------------------------------
Subtotal                       $417,927,000
```

This is **not yet the final taxpayer and business-paid intake total**. It is a source-backed starting subtotal only.

Why not call it final yet?

- The $110.594 million capital-grants-and-contributions category can include developer-paid impact fees, connection fees, installation fees, and outside grants.
- The ACFR says business-type capital grant/contribution changes include connection-fee and installation-fee revenue, but the category is still mixed.
- The governmental-fund statement separately reports $15.670 million in impact fees.
- TRACE must avoid both omissions and double-counting when moving between government-wide and fund statements.

## Why the Existing Operating-Fund Control Is Different

The existing TRACE operating-fund control is:

| Control | Amount | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Q4 operating-fund revenue | $446,683,216 | Eight operating funds; unaudited year-end report |
| ACFR citywide revenue | $616,360,000 | Citywide government-wide financial reporting; audited, rounded to thousands in the summary table |

These are not supposed to equal each other. They use different accounting scopes and classifications. The Q4 total includes an eight-fund operating slice; the audited ACFR covers governmental and business-type activities citywide and presents revenue on the government-wide accrual basis.
