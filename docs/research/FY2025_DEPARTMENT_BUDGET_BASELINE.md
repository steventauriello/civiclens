# FY 2024-25 Department Budget Baseline

**Status:** Research draft - adopted-budget baseline only  
**Fiscal year:** October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2025  
**Primary source:** [City of Port St. Lucie FY 2024-25 Adopted Budget Book](https://www.cityofpsl.com/files/assets/public/v/7/departments/office-of-management-amp-budget/documents/fy-24-25-adopted-budget.pdf)

## What this establishes

This is TRACE's first department-level baseline. Each figure below is the City's **FY 2024-25 adopted budget**, not actual spending and not a claim that the amount has been transaction-traced.

The department registry and the budget book do not use exactly the same organizational level. Some public-facing offices are accounted for inside a parent department. TRACE will preserve that relationship rather than assigning the same money twice.

## Reconciliation rules

1. **Adopted budget is not actual spending.** Actuals require Q4 and/or ACFR-supported records.
2. **Parent and child rows cannot be summed together.** Police plus Animal Control, for example, would double count.
3. **Funds remain distinct.** General Fund taxes, enterprise fees, CRA/TIF, grants, capital funds, and internal-service funds will not be presented as one interchangeable pot.
4. **No department trace percentage yet.** A department percentage starts only after its actual-spending control total and mapped supporting records are available.
5. **Public labels must say scope.** For example: “FY 2024-25 adopted General Fund department budget,” not “all Port St. Lucie spending.”
