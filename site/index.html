Accountability • Effectiveness • Need

AEN evidence reviews

A structured review of what was authorized, what happened, and whether the public record establishes a genuine need.

AEN

A review—not an accusation

Evidence should lead. Conclusions should follow.

TRACE separates confirmed facts, calculations, agency claims, contradictory evidence, and unresolved gaps. Missing data is never presented as proof of misconduct.

01

Accountability

Trace authority, funding, contracts, changes, payments, responsible officials, and audit findings.

  • Who approved the expenditure?
  • What controls and reporting were required?
  • Did actual spending reconcile to the record?
02

Effectiveness

Compare promised outputs and public outcomes with measurable, time-bound evidence.

  • Were objectives defined before spending?
  • What result was delivered?
  • Was the result independently verified?
03

Need

Examine the public problem, available alternatives, legal obligations, demand, and fiscal tradeoffs.

  • What need was documented?
  • Were lower-cost options considered?
  • Why was this funding level necessary?
